## Phase 10 — Pipelines-first, content-thin (2026-05-06 PM reframe)

**Framing:** the assets being produced today are mostly throwaway proofs of pipeline correctness — except possibly the characters. The point is figuring out which pipelines work, not building a content library. Quality bar still high; volume bar dropped to "enough to prove the pipeline."
